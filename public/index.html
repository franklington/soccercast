SoccerCast

Multiplayer Football · Chromecast-ready

📺 TV / Receiver Open this on your TV, Chromecast browser, or any big screen. 📱 Controller Open this on your phone to join and play with the virtual joystick.

How to play

1. Open TV / Receiver in a browser on your TV or cast a tab
2. Each player opens Controller on their phone
3. Enter a name, pick Red or Blue team & hit Join
4. Use the joystick to move — run into the ball to kick it!
5. Score in the opponent's goal to win 🏆
